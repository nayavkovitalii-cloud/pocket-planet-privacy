Privacy Policy

Pocket Planet: Idle World · Android mobile game

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent developer of Pocket Planet: Idle World (the “Game”) handles information when you play the Game or contact us. For privacy inquiries, email nayavko.vitalii@gmail.com.

1. Game accounts and locally stored progress

The Game does not require registration or sign-in and does not ask players to provide their name, email address, or phone number to play. Game progress, preferences, achievements, and other gameplay information are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a game-account server or receive players’ local progress automatically.

Depending on your Android settings, operating-system backup services may back up app data separately under their own terms. You can delete local progress in the Game using Settings → Reset all progress or remove local app data by uninstalling the Game. The Game also lets you copy a backup code; you control where you paste or share that code.

2. Advertising and third-party data processing

The Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads, including banner ads in menus and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process the following information:

  • IP address and approximate location: an IP address can be used to estimate a general location. The Game does not request precise GPS location for advertising.
  • App and ad interactions: app launches, interactions, ad views, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics: app and SDK performance information, including launch times, hanging, and energy usage.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device identifiers.

These data are collected and shared by the advertising SDK for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Advertising data may be handled by Google and relevant advertising partners in accordance with their policies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps and sites that use its services.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent and privacy choices for advertising. Where the option is available, you can review or change your choice under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Opting out of personalised advertising does not necessarily stop all technical processing necessary for ads, security, or measurement.

You may also reset or delete your Android advertising ID using your device's privacy settings. Rewarded video ads are optional; you do not need to view one to access the core game.

4. Contacting the developer

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use this to reply to support and privacy inquiries. Such correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain a record of the request; you may ask us to delete support correspondence where applicable.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience of teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in the Game. Parents or guardians with questions can contact the address below.

6. Security, retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until it is reset, cleared or removed, subject to any device-level backups. We do not retain copies of gameplay progress on our own servers. Advertising information collected by Google is subject to Google's retention, security and deletion policies. Advertising SDK communication is encrypted in transit.

For information about advertising data or requests concerning information processed by Google, refer to Google's Privacy Policy. You can contact us for help understanding how this applies to the Game.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information. To ask about data handled directly by the developer, contact us using the email below. Requests concerning information held by Google may also be directed to Google.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the Game, its services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the most recent update. The currently published version will remain available on this page.
